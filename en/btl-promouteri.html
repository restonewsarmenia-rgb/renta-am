Questions

What people ask most often

How is BTL work paid for?

Promotional work is billed differently. We draw up the agreement around your brief, and payment goes by bank transfer to the company. The price follows the campaign: the format, the number of people, the number of shifts. The placement fee set out above – 50 % of a month’s salary, six months, up to three free replacements – applies where you take a member of the promotional team onto your own payroll.

Can we book a single promoter?

Yes, though a team usually works out better: one agreement and one supervisor cover several sites or shifts. We will suggest the right number for the job.

Do you supply uniform and printed material?

We arrange it through the agency’s partners – uniform made up, material printed. It is a separate line in the estimate, and it saves you finding contractors.

What reporting do we get?

Interim and final reports with photographs and video, plus figures on how the campaign performed: contacts made, samples handed out, what shoppers said.

What if someone does not turn up?

The supervisor checks attendance. If a person fails to appear we cover the shift with a replacement – that is our responsibility, not your problem.
