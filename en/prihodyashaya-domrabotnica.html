Questions

What people ask most often

What does a visiting housekeeper cost in Yerevan?

It depends on the floor area, how often she comes and what is on the list: a routine clean costs less than a deep clean with the windows and the carpets. Our fee is 50 % of one month’s salary, of which the client pays 25 %. We will give you figures after a short conversation about the flat and the days.

Why not simply answer an advert?

Finding someone yourself carries four risks: poor work, with ruined fabric or a broken machine and no one to claim against; someone who stops turning up without a word; no check on documents or references; and nothing in writing. The agency takes those risks off you and answers for the work.

How often can she come?

From once a week to three times. One-off visits are possible too – a deep clean, or clearing up after an event. We agree the days in advance and write them into the agreement.

What if she falls ill?

We tell you in advance about any change to the days and find a replacement quickly. While the agreement runs, replacements are free – up to three of them.

Do we have to buy the cleaning products?

As a rule the client provides the products and the equipment, and she chooses the right product for each surface. If you would rather she bought them herself, that goes into the agreement separately.
