For candidates

Work in homes we have vetted

We place staff with families and companies, and we find work for household staff. A written agreement, hours agreed in advance, and the agency behind you.

Requirements

Who we take on our books

We do not send inexperienced people to a client. Someone who cannot do the job causes a family more problems than they solve.

  • Experience in the role, and a way for us to confirm it
  • Passport, health certificate and criminal record certificate
  • References from previous employers – we ring the referees ourselves
  • Carers: a nursing qualification
  • Governesses: a degree in education or psychology
  • Care with other people’s things, punctuality, and respect for the family’s privacy
Terms

What you get

  • Households and companies we have checked – the client is vetted too
  • An agreement: your hours and your duties written down
  • A standard working day of 6–8 hours, with overtime at an hourly rate
  • The agency behind you for the whole term – awkward conversations are ours
  • Our fee to you is 25 % of your first month’s salary, deducted once
