Questions

What people ask most often

What does a housekeeper cost in Yerevan?

The salary follows the market and the size of the job: the floor area, how often you want cleaning, whether she lives in, extras such as the shopping and the pets. Our fee is worked out from that salary – 50 % of one month, of which the client pays 25 %. We will name the range at the first consultation.

Can a housekeeper live in?

Yes. She lives with the family and is there through the day. The living arrangements, the days off and overtime go into the agreement beside the list of duties.

Do you check candidates?

Every candidate goes through five stages: documents (passport, health certificate and a criminal record certificate), calls to previous employers, an assessment of character, an interview in person and a trial period. A profile reaches you only after that.

How long does the search take?

First profiles come 3–5 days after the agreement is signed, usually three to five candidates. A narrow brief takes longer – a live-in housekeeper who has looked after antiques, for instance.

What if the housekeeper does not suit?

The agreement allows up to three free replacements. If the temperament, the hours or the standard of work turns out wrong, we find someone else and you pay nothing more.
