Duties

What a housemaid takes on

This is more than mopping floors. A trained housemaid keeps the whole house to a standard.

  • Cleaning throughout: rooms, kitchen, bathrooms and the utility rooms
  • Furniture, carpets and floors
  • Changing bed linen and towels
  • Taking the linen to the laundry and checking what comes back
  • Looking after the plants
  • Professional cleaning products matched to each surface, not one bottle for everything
  • Keeping an eye on supplies and order in the store cupboards

Questions

What people ask most often

What does a housemaid cost in Yerevan?

The salary depends on the size of the house, the schedule, whether she lives in and what is on the list. Our fee is 50 % of one month’s salary, of which the client pays 25 %. We will give you a salary range at the first consultation, once we know the scale of the work.

How is a housemaid different from a housekeeper?

A housemaid is there for cleanliness and order: the cleaning, the bed linen, furniture and floors, the laundry. A housekeeper takes a wider round of the house – washing and ironing, the shopping, errands, sometimes cooking. We put the boundary in the agreement.

Is there a trial period?

Yes, 5 working days. That is usually long enough to see whether she suits your house and the pace of it.

What if she is ill or away on holiday?

We find a replacement at no extra cost: while the agreement runs there are up to three free replacements. Any change to the schedule we tell you about in advance.

Do you help with the paperwork?

Yes. We handle the legal side of the employment and advise on working matters for the whole term of the agreement.
