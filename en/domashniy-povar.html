Duties

What a private chef does

This is more than somebody who cooks. The chef takes charge of how the household eats: what everyone likes, when they eat, and what their health allows.

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner, to a menu you have agreed
  • Planning the week’s food around what the family likes
  • Buying the food, fresh and in season
  • Checking use-by dates and keeping the store cupboard stocked
  • Leaving the kitchen clean
  • Laying the table and presenting the food well
  • A prescribed or therapeutic diet
  • A children’s menu by age, and around whatever the child cannot eat
  • Cooking for a party, and putting together a banquet menu

Questions

What people ask most often

Can we taste the chef’s cooking before we hire?

Yes, and we insist on it. The chef cooks several dishes in your own kitchen, and you judge the food, the way it is served and the way they work. A trial period of a few days follows, and you are not committed to anything.

What does a private chef cost in Yerevan?

It depends on the arrangement and the speciality. A live-out chef three days a week costs a good deal less than a live-in one, or a chef who cooks therapeutic diets. Our fee is 50 % of one month’s salary, of which the client pays 25 %.

Does the chef do the shopping?

Usually, yes. It is part of the job: choosing what is fresh and in season, checking use-by dates, keeping the store cupboard well stocked. The budget and how you settle it are agreed beforehand and written into the agreement.

Can we have a chef only for a party?

Yes. A single evening – a banquet, a family gathering – is a separate arrangement, from a small dinner to a table for 50 guests and more. The menu is agreed in advance.

Will the chef work around diets and allergies?

Yes. We look for a chef whose speciality fits: therapeutic diets set by a doctor, children’s menus around allergies, vegetarian cooking. We go through the restrictions at the first consultation.
