Arrangements

The kinds of nanny families ask for

The arrangement depends on the family’s hours and the age of the child, from a few hours a day to a nanny who lives in.

Daily nanny

Works to a schedule: a few hours a day, or certain days of the week.

Live-in nanny

Lives with the family and is there through the day. Suits parents with long or unpredictable hours.

Night nanny

Helps with a newborn: night feeds, settling, getting sleep back on track.

Governess

Care and teaching: languages, schoolwork and getting ready for school.

Duties

What the work covers

The duties go into the agreement before the nanny starts, so nothing is left to guesswork.

  • Looking after the child: meals, bathing, bedtime
  • Time outdoors every day
  • Games, reading, drawing and other things that teach without feeling like lessons
  • The nursery run, the school run, clubs and doctor’s appointments
  • Help with homework
  • Keeping the child’s room tidy
  • Simple cooking for the child

Questions

What people ask most often

What does a nanny cost in Yerevan?

The nanny’s salary follows the market and what you ask for: the hours, whether the nanny lives in, languages, the age of the child. Our fee is worked out from it – 50 % of one month’s salary, of which the client pays 25 %. We will give you a salary range at the first consultation, once we understand the job.

Can we have a live-in nanny?

Yes. A live-in nanny is in the house and available through the day, which suits parents with long hours. The living arrangements, days off and overtime all go into the agreement.

Do you check candidates?

Every candidate goes through four stages: documents (passport, employment record, health certificate, criminal record certificate), an interview in person, calls to previous employers, and a check of practical skills. A profile reaches you only after all four.

How long does the search take?

First profiles come 3–5 days after the agreement is signed, usually three to five candidates so there is a choice. A narrow brief takes longer – a nanny with English who also lives in, for instance.

Can we ask for someone else?

Yes. The agreement allows up to three free replacements. If the nanny is not right for you – the temperament, the hours, the standard of work – we find another at no cost to you.
