Types of care

The carers we place

What is needed depends on health and on how much they can still do for themselves.

Companion carer

For someone still largely independent who wants company, a hand about the house and someone in the flat.

Nursing carer

After a stroke, with serious long-term illness, and through rehabilitation.

Live-out

Works to a schedule: a few hours a day, or in shifts.

Live-in

In the house all the time, with care and someone keeping an eye on them round the clock.

Duties

What the work covers

We set the list of tasks for each household. It follows the person’s health and the shape of their day.

  • Washing, bathing and skin care
  • Cooking to the prescribed diet and to what they actually like
  • Keeping the room clean
  • Walks out, and trips to appointments
  • Shopping and errands
  • Making sure medicines are taken on time
  • Blood pressure, temperature and blood sugar readings
  • Help moving about, and preventing pressure sores
  • Conversation, reading aloud, cards and walks

Questions

What people ask most often

What does a carer cost in Yerevan?

It depends on the arrangement and on how much care the person needs. A companion for a few hours costs a good deal less than a live-in nursing carer. Our fee is worked out from the carer’s monthly salary – 50 % of one month, of which the client pays 25 %. We will give you a range once we know the person’s condition.

Does the carer need a nursing qualification?

If there is a diagnosis that calls for procedures, readings to take or help with moving about, then yes. If the person manages alone and mainly wants somebody there to talk to, a companion carer is enough.

How quickly can you find someone?

First profiles come 3–5 days after the agreement is signed. When it is urgent we move faster. Say so when you ring: some of our candidates can start at short notice.

What if the carer is not the right person?

It happens, and the sooner you tell us the better. When someone is frail, whether they get on with the carer matters more than anything else. The agreement allows up to three free replacements while it runs.

Do you stay in touch after the carer starts?

Yes. We stay with the family for the whole term of the agreement: adjusting the schedule, sorting out disagreements, arranging cover when the carer is ill or on holiday.
