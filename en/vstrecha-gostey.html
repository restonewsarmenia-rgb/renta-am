Who we place

Four roles, from a supplier dropping in to a state delegation

Meeting a supplier and looking after a foreign delegation are different jobs. We put the team together for the particular visit.

Host or reception assistant

Meets people at the door, takes them to the meeting room, offers drinks, makes the welcome feel meant.

Personal guide

Stays with a guest or a delegation for the whole visit: logistics, messages, whatever goes wrong. Where the visit calls for it, the same person drives.

Interpreter and guide

Language support and coordination of the visit in one person. Armenian, Russian, English.

Protocol assistant

For official delegations and senior visits: business etiquette, composure, high standards.

Questions

What people ask most often

Can we book someone for a single visit?

Yes, and it is the most common request. A single booking covers one date and one guest. We also place staff for visits of several days and for permanent reception work.

How fast can you find someone?

Normally 3–5 days. If a partner arrives tomorrow, say so when you write and we work faster.

Do we need a separate interpreter?

Not necessarily. An interpreter who also runs the visit covers the language and the logistics at once, which costs less than hiring two people.

What is your fee?

For a one-off event we quote against the brief: the format, the length and the standing of the guest all count. For permanent staff the usual arrangement applies: 50 % of the monthly salary, of which the client pays 25 %.

What about confidentiality?

What we learn about your guests, your partners and your talks stays inside the agency. For a protocol visit that is a condition, not a courtesy.
