Questions

What people ask most often

How is this different from a cleaning company?

A cleaning company sends a crew on a rota and the faces change. We find one cleaner attached to your office: someone who knows your requirements, remembers what must not be touched, and does not need it explained each time.

What is your fee?

It depends on the arrangement – an hour a day, half a day or a full day. Our fee is 50 % of the employee’s monthly salary, of which the client pays 25 %.

How quickly will you find someone?

3–5 days from signing. We know the market district by district and look for a candidate who lives near your office – and it shows in their timekeeping.

What if the person falls ill?

We cover the post with a temporary replacement and the office carries on as usual. Within the guarantee period the replacement costs you nothing.

Can we combine the cleaning with other work?

Yes – that is the assistant with a wider brief: cleaning plus greeting visitors, help with papers and small errands. The range of duties goes into the agreement.
